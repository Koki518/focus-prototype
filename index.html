Sensor test

Does face-down work on this phone?

Tap Start, lay the phone face-up on a table, then flip it face-down. Repeat 5 times. You should hear “Face down” when the phone is detected face-down and “Face up” when you pick it up (beeps if this phone cannot speak). Then hold the phone face-down in your hand, for example above your face, for 5 seconds: it should say “Phone moved” and must not count as face-down.
